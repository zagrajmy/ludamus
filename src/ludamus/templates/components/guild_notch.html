{% comment %} The guild mark as a notch tucked into a presenter's avatar. Owns one decision: which corner the notch takes. The avatar's own warning badge (components/avatar.html, shown when the viewer has shadowbanned that person) sits in the bottom-right, so when it is present the notch takes the bottom-left. The notch mirrors that badge: `size-3.5` against the badge's `size-2.5` icon in `p-0.5`, both 14px squircles with the same knockout ring, so a flagged avatar reads as a matched pair rather than a disc beside a triangle. Both hang 5px off the avatar, sitting on its border rather than tucked inside it. Dead centre on that border is about 4px: the avatar is a squircle, so its edge at 45° runs closer to the middle than a circle's would — with the global `corner-shape: superellipse(1.8)` the exponent is 2**1.8 ≈ 3.48, putting that point at 2**(-1/3.48) ≈ 0.819 of the half-width, so centring a 14px box there wants an overhang of 7 - 0.181 * half-width, i.e. 4.1px on the card's `size-8` avatar and 3.7px on the modal's `size-9`. 5px is a pixel further out than that on purpose: dead centre, the knockout ring bites into the avatar enough to read as a notch cut out of it, and the extra pixel makes the decoration read as sitting on top instead. One value covers both avatar sizes. That overhang only renders if the row holding the avatar lets it: a row that clips its horizontal overflow shaves the left-hand notch, because its left edge sits on the avatar's. Rows that hold a notch bound their own contents instead of clipping — see the presenter row in _session_card.html. Surfaces that render the mark beside the name rather than on the avatar (_compact_session_row.html, _room_lane_tile.html) include guild_mark.html directly — there is no corner to contend for there.
