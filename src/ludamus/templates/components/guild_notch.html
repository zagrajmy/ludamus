{% comment %} The guild mark as a notch tucked into a presenter's avatar. Owns one decision: which corner the notch takes. The avatar's own warning badge (components/avatar.html, shown when the viewer has shadowbanned that person) sits in the bottom-right, so when it is present the notch takes the bottom-left. The notch mirrors that badge deliberately — `size-3.5` and a 0.5 inset against the badge's `size-2.5` icon in `p-0.5` at `-bottom-0.5 -right-0.5`. Both are 14x14 boxes hanging 2px off the avatar, so a flagged avatar reads as a matched pair rather than a large disc beside a small triangle. The insets are not quite mirror images, and that is deliberate. The rows that hold the avatar are `overflow-hidden` — they stop a long presenter name spilling the card — and their left edge sits exactly on the avatar's, so anything hanging off to the left is cut while the same overhang on the right has 300-odd px of row to spill into. So the bottom-left notch sits flush (`left-0`) and only the bottom-right one hangs (`-right-0.5`). Both then lose the same 2px off the bottom that the badge has always lost, and neither shows a shaved edge. Giving those rows room to render the decorations whole is a change to card layout, not to this component. Surfaces that render the mark beside the name rather than on the avatar (_compact_session_row.html, _room_lane_tile.html) include guild_mark.html directly — there is no corner to contend for there.
