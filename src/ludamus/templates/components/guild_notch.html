The avatar's own warning badge (components/avatar.html, shown when the viewer has shadowbanned that person) sits in the bottom-right, so when it is present the notch moves to the bottom-left and shrinks. It has to shrink as well as move. Measured in a browser: a full-size 1.35rem notch on a size-8 avatar spans x=123→144.6 while the badge starts at x=143, so opposite corners alone still overlap by 1.6px across the badge's full height. At size-4 they clear by 4px, and size-4 is the size the mark already takes on the compact row and the room lane, so it is not a new dimension. Surfaces that render the mark beside the name rather than on the avatar (_compact_session_row.html, _room_lane_tile.html) include guild_mark.html directly — there is no corner to contend for there.
