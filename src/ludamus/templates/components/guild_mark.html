{% load i18n %} {% comment %} A guild's uploaded mark on a white plate. The plate is a squircle, inheriting the global `corner-shape: superellipse(1.8)` from index.css rather than opting out of it. On an avatar it sits next to the warning badge (components/avatar.html), which is the same 14px box with the same knockout ring, so sharing the corner shape makes the two read as a matched pair instead of two unrelated stickers. The plate is white in both themes: an uploaded mark is often a dark silhouette on transparency, which would vanish against the dark card surface. The image is inset to the plate's inscribed square so a full-bleed square upload keeps its corners instead of being clipped by the curve. For a superellipse the exponent is 2**1.8 ≈ 3.48, so the inscribed square runs to (1/2)**(1/3.48) ≈ 0.82 of the box — wider than a circle's 0.707, which is why this is 82% and not the 72% a round plate needed. Renders nothing when the guild has no mark yet — the feature is the mark, and two-letter initials at this size are illegible. Variables: guild needs .name and .logo_url size Tailwind size-* class.
