{% load i18n %} {% comment %} A guild's uploaded mark on a white plate. The plate is a squircle, inheriting the global `corner-shape: superellipse(1.8)` from index.css rather than opting out of it. On an avatar it sits next to the warning badge (components/avatar.html), which is the same 14px box with the same knockout ring, so sharing the corner shape makes the two read as a matched pair instead of two unrelated stickers. The plate is white in both themes: an uploaded mark is often a dark silhouette on transparency, which would vanish against the dark card surface. The image fills the plate so full-bleed uploads visibly inherit its squircle. `object-contain` keeps non-square marks uncropped. `title` names the guild on hover: at this size the mark alone is a puzzle for anyone who does not already know the crest. It matches `alt` word for word, so a screen reader that reads both does not read two different things. On a surface where a full-bleed link covers the card, the caller has to lift the mark above it (`z-20 pointer-events-auto`) or nothing ever hovers the image — see guild_notch.html. Renders nothing when the guild has no mark yet — the feature is the mark, and two-letter initials at this size are illegible. Variables: guild needs .name and .logo_url size Tailwind size-* class.
