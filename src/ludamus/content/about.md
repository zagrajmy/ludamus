# About Ludamus

Ludamus is a community platform for tabletop gaming events.

## What we offer

- Event scheduling and management
- Session proposals from game masters
- Player enrollment system
- Anonymous participation options

## Our Mission

We believe that tabletop gaming brings people together. Whether you're rolling
dice in a dungeon crawl, negotiating trades in a strategy game, or weaving
stories in a narrative RPG, we're here to help you find your table.
